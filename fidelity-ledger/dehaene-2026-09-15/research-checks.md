# Selected research checks

Checked 2026-09-15. This is a bounded supplement, not a systematic search or a full-source reading claim. Abstract-level use is limited to the claims stated there; failed full-text requests were not treated as successful reads.

| Source | Inspected basis and contribution |
|---|---|
| [COGITATE Consortium 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08888-1) | Primary abstract and article sections on preregistration, 256 participants, onset/offset, and interpretation. Constrains tested GNWT/IIT predictions, no winner by fiat. |
| [Cohen et al. 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32409620/) | Primary abstract and accessible figure descriptions. PMC full-text requests encountered a browser check; no claim of full methodological audit. P3b/report dissociation is retained with no-report limits. |
| [Dehaene & Cohen 2007](https://www.unicog.org/publications/DehaeneCohen_CulturalRecyclingCorticalMaps_Neuron2007.pdf) | Author-hosted theory statement/abstract, corroborated by supplied 2009 book. Not an independent experiment. |
| [Saygin et al. 2016](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5003691/) | Primary article on age-5 connectivity predicting age-8 selectivity, including prereader comparisons and leave-one-out prediction. Not causal sufficiency. |
| [Doust et al. 2022](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41588-022-01192-y) | Primary abstract, population/self-report design, small-locus-effect description; candidate gene/pathway methods inspected. No gene-panel or migration diagnosis. |
| [Xu & Spelke 2000](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10594312/) | Primary abstract: six-month-old large-number discrimination, 8:16 versus 8:12 under controls. No infant symbolic subtraction inference. |
| [Dehaene et al. 2003](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20957581/) | Authors' abstract: explicitly tentative quantity/verbal/attention tripartite parietal account; kept separate from triple representational codes. |
| [Dehaene & Cohen 1997](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9220256/) | Primary abstract: two acquired cases, rote verbal versus quantitative dissociation. |
| [Dehaene 2000 chapter](https://www.unicog.org/publications/DehaeneS2000CerebralBasesInNeuroscience.pdf) | Author-hosted indexed framework terminology only; not a full added-source extraction. |
| [International Dyslexia Association](https://dyslexiaida.org/testing-and-evaluation/) | Professional association's evaluation/support guidance, especially evaluation purpose and qualified interpretation. Used only for general next-step guidance; not a current local eligibility/diagnostic-rule audit. Added after a behavioral probe omitted a useful next action. |

The primary-study passages are short paraphrases. They do not imply that every study cited inside the four books was checked. Dates identify the paper/edition, not web crawl dates. Research cited here retains its original rights and is not republished as raw text.
