# Coverage and fidelity audit

## Purpose and reading boundary

The acceptance suite was frozen in staging before semantic framework extraction and reference writing. Source metadata and tables of contents supplied the structural plan: seven chapters plus introduction for What Is Health?, fifteen chapters plus the closing ten-principle list for Principles of Neural Design. Semantic reading developed the inventory below. This is an argument-wide, sampled distillation, not a claim that every page, figure, citation, or named protein was verified.

Original Markdown line ranges are portable within the source hashes in source-provenance.json. Converted heading counts (zero canonical chapters) were rejected in favor of the contents pages. The extractor’s 315/746 page estimates are length heuristics, not editions’ page counts. The references describe numbered argument spans instead. The 477,725 combined raw-token estimate is similarly a planning estimate affected by source formatting; it is not this run’s usage.

## Samples outside the opening principle inventory

These passages were selected because they could defeat a simplistic application, rather than merely repeat a named principle. They were incorporated before final editorial inspection.

1. **Neural Design 3159–3180 (chapter 6, pp. 135–136):** a component can operate above its efficiency optimum for robustness, and whole-system matching can justify extra downstream capacity. This changes an otherwise misleading “minimize everything” answer. Retained in chapter 6, core, and decisions. Eight lines overlap an earlier slice; this was targeted verification after truncated output, not a second chapter read.
2. **Neural Design 9316–9365 (chapter 14, pp. 427–429):** expected rewards can remain pleasurable despite zero reward-prediction error. Retained next to the learning model and in the scope boundary. This prevents collapsing meaning or all pleasure into phasic dopamine.
3. **Neural Design 6250–6308 (chapter 10, pp. 273–276):** vestibular bidirectional signals justify costly tonic firing and oppose blanket rectification/sparsity. Retained with the method, not isolated in the ledger.
4. **Health 1263–1407 (chapter 2, pp. 38–43):** averaged cardiac ATP recordings leave individual-cell constancy uncertain; prediction needs correction and reserves. The measurement caveat and feedback role survive. The particular mitochondrial control model is source-specific.
5. **Health 4295–4322 (within chapter 7 read, pp. 173–174):** highest-level therapy is not a universal call to replace pharmacotherapy. Retained beside his intervention principle, counterbalancing the more sweeping rhetoric of chapter 6.
6. **Health 3707–3787 (chapter 6, pp. 147–150):** an additional metabolism check exposed the leptin-sign discrepancy. Expanded the audit to primary research before preserving the multiorgan framework. Kept a conspicuous runtime correction; did not silently fix the source or claim to know whether the book or conversion caused it.

## Fidelity and editorial review

The expert’s core changes its unit of inquiry and outcome criteria, rather than appending author biographies. It keeps mechanistic explanation, causal identification, measurement, competing models, retention/transfer, and individual-diagnosis boundaries. Runtime claims distinguish book mechanisms, author hypotheses, evolutionary readings, editorial application, external checks, and normative choice.

The references are below their whole-file caps and planning ceilings. Neural Design is deliberately below its chapter-count target: the fifteen-section spine is retained while redundant illustrations and full anatomical inventories are compressed. Token density is not certified by counting bold labels as independent frameworks; each retained item was reviewed for its decision consequence and conditions. This is editorial coverage evidence, not proof of model performance.

The learning-app example remains marked as a proposed application. Source estimates and selected source-span coverage do not imply a full read. Framework routing and written judgments demonstrate a content change; fresh-context behavior has not been measured.
