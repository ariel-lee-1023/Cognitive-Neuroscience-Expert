## Development inspection

- **Learning app:** the core and Health reference distinguish score gains, retention/transfer, demands outside the task, anticipatory responses, recovery time, and participation. The example calls for measured alternatives and does not infer an endocrine mechanism from anxiety.
- **Higher firing:** Neural Design specifies signal/task/latency, marginal information, fixed and variable costs, geometry and population size. It separates capacity, actual coding, and animal use.

- **Individual prognosis:** decision rules supply no cortisol threshold or lifespan calculator. The user would need relevant individual and longitudinal evidence.

## Final editorial inspection
