# Coverage and fidelity audit — four-book extension

## Scope and method

The four capability gaps in the user's image governed selection. Each book has one canonical reference, one reconstructed worked example, source-specific method conditions, and decision rules. Chapter counts were checked against headings/contents instead of trusting the extractor's low counts: Craver 7, Dayan–Abbott 10, Hernán–Robins 22, Luck 8. The extractor reported only 2, 2, 2, and 10 respectively. Token-derived page estimates are labeled as estimates, not original pagination.

This is targeted structural distillation, not exhaustive semantic coverage of all source lines. The reading ledger records cleaned source-local slices. Some large combined tool responses were truncated, so submitted slice-token totals are conservative upper estimates of visible source input, not exact provider tokens or complete inspection. Targeted rereads resolved the crucial Craver mutual-manipulability qualifications. Craver exceeded the provisional four-times reading-budget alert because of long source lines and rereads; expenditure was reviewed and stopped after the relevance/organization distinctions were resolved. No whole-book reload was used.

## Attribution and integration decisions

- Craver's stronger standard for an actual mechanism coexists with Dayan–Abbott's descriptive and interpretive models. The core neither rejects useful abstractions nor labels their success biological proof.
- Mechanistic component–whole levels are not equated with computational/algorithmic/implementation distinctions.
- Dayan–Abbott's generative-model use of “causal” is not imported as identification of intervention effects. Hernán–Robins supplies a different assumption framework.
- Controlled and natural direct effects retain the source's distinction; identifying a total effect does not establish mediation or a full mechanism.
- Luck's p-value statement is corrected openly rather than silently attributed to the author. Prospective/independent window selection and broader inferential guardrails are editorial integration with the existing skill, not claims that this edition presents every modern practice.
- The Luck edition mismatch is unresolved at the source-availability level and is explicit in the reference, core, README, and provenance. No material from the unsupplied second edition was invented.
- Existing reference files and local demonstration/maintenance files were preserved byte-for-byte. Behavioral gains and controlled pre/post non-regression are not claimed; fresh-context runs are unrun.
