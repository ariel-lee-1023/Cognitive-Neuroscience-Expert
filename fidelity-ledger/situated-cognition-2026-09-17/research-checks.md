# Selected evidence checks

Checked 2026-09-17. This is a bounded primary-study check plus one research synthesis, not a systematic search, comprehensive current consensus, or effect-size reanalysis. Abstract-level checks support only the limited claims stated in the runtime. Some PMC opens returned access checks; PubMed, publisher, and author-institution records supplied usable evidence. No full-text review is implied where only the abstract was inspected.

| Claim / source | Checked evidence and access depth | Change in the reference |
|---|---|---|
| Writing prevents choking | [Myers, Davis & Chan, 2021](https://doi.org/10.1186/s41235-021-00309-x), publisher abstract and introductory discussion | Null anxiety/performance results across the tested college exams preclude a guaranteed benefit; does not disprove every writing protocol |
| Gesture helps learning because it is bodily | [Congdon et al., 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29051690/), abstract and indexed methods/conclusion | Timing and complementary information matter; distinguish simultaneous from sequential speech/gesture in the studied third-graders |
| Action–sentence compatibility as settled support for embodiment | [Morey et al., online 2021 / volume 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34755319/), primary abstract | Null pooled result for the tested protocol; not a refutation of all grounding or a direct test of gesture teaching |
| Pencil/smile induction establishes general mood benefit | [Many Smiles, 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36266452/), primary indexed abstract | Mimicry/voluntary-expression evidence distinguished from less conclusive pen-in-mouth evidence; no clinical extrapolation |
| Warm objects improve social judgment | [Chabris et al., 2019](https://doi.org/10.1027/1864-9335/a000361), author-institution abstract at Geisinger | Larger double-blind field replications with near-zero effects; warmth is not promoted as a persuasion technique |
| Power posture produces hormonal/risk-taking changes | [Ranehill et al., 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25810452/), indexed primary record/title | Endpoint-specific failure; no claim that all postural or subjective effects vanish |
| Every retained item requires sustained decodable activity | [Rose et al., 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27934762/), primary abstract | Reactivation of potentially relevant latent information qualifies exclusivity; does not uniquely establish synaptic mechanism |
| Adaptive n-back gives broad ability gains | [Redick et al., 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22708717/), primary abstract | Trained gains without positive transfer to tested cognitive abilities; identify control and population |
| Broader training literature | [Melby-Lervåg, Redick & Hulme, 2016](https://doi.org/10.1177/1745691616635612), abstract and indexed results | Contextual synthesis of limited far transfer against treated controls, not a primary single experiment |
| Greater training dose necessarily improves transfer | [Zhong et al., 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40914122/), primary abstract | Dose-related practiced gains with no observed near/far transfer in that preregistered active-control study |

Further questions requiring additional retrieval: efficacy of a particular current product; exercise dosage and long-term cognitive effects; present clinical guidance; individual gesture/accessibility adaptations; the complete replication record of pressure interventions and social priming. No book source is silently relabeled as contemporary consensus.
