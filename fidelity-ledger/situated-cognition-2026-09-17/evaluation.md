## Final editorial inspection, after draft integration

The final partition was inspected once against the fixed candidate; it is not an independent holdout because the author created the prompts and had them in context.

- Musician: core/reference preserve expertise for the particular passage, distinguish strategic attention and mechanics monitoring, and name fatigue/equipment/physical alternatives. A successful cue is evidence of benefit, not unique proof of mechanism.
- Warm mug/posture: Body reference carries endpoint-specific primary evidence checks and avoids promising hiring or clinical benefits. Absence of these effects is not generalized to all embodiment.
- Office support versus training: Klingberg table separates demand, state externalization, domain practice, and putative capacity training; costs, missed urgent events and delayed transfer remain distinct.
- Individual imaging/medication: core maintains group-versus-individual and clinical scope boundaries; situational overload is not automatically ADHD.
