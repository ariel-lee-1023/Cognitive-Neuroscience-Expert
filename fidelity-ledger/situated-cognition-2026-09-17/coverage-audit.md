## Cross-source synthesis and fidelity boundaries

The differential-diagnosis table, workplace resumption protocol, accessibility adaptations, and four-way intervention comparison are editorial synthesis. They are not a named protocol from any single book or an independently validated individual diagnostic instrument. Existing mechanistic/causal standards remain in the core; the new material changes what questions it asks about actual performance.

A positive intervention effect can justify a useful design without uniquely identifying its mediator. Assisted completion, retained task learning, broader transfer, and reported well-being are separate outcomes. The source's popular metaphors—horsepower, mental muscles, mirror systems, seven items—are not treated as measurements.
